OUR JOURNEY

A Small Curiosity That Became Something Meaningful

Whimsy & Weave began with simple curiosity and a quiet love for handmade art. What started as experimenting with yarn slowly became a comforting little escape filled with creativity, colors, and late-night crocheting sessions.

Stitch by stitch, small handmade gifts turned into products filled with warmth and personality. The excitement of receiving the first few orders brought confidence, happiness, and the realization that something meaningful was slowly growing.

Through support, trust, and countless hours of learning, Whimsy & Weave became more than just a hobby. It became a dream that helped build confidence, independence, and a beautiful little handmade world created with love.

MEET THE FOUNDER

The Hands Behind Every Stitch

Hi, I’m Pragathi — the creator, designer, packer, photographer, and creative mind behind Whimsy & Weave.

Every product you see is made with patience, warmth, and attention to detail. From choosing yarn shades to carefully packing each order, every little part of the process is done personally with love and gratitude.

Balancing creativity, learning, business, and everyday life hasn’t always been easy, but crochet became a safe space that slowly transformed into something beautiful and meaningful.

SUSTAINABILITY

Handmade With Care For You & The Planet

Whimsy & Weave believes that handmade creations should feel comforting, safe, and thoughtful for both customers and the environment.

We use eco-friendly packaging materials, recyclable packaging, and focus on reducing unnecessary plastic wherever possible.

Slowly transitioning from acrylic yarn to cotton yarn has also become an important step — making products softer, more breathable, skin-friendly, and environmentally conscious.

GRATITUDE

None Of This Would Exist Without The People Around Me

Whimsy & Weave grew because of the love, encouragement, and support from friends, family, and every single customer who believed in a small handmade dream.

From calming nervousness during stalls and campus events, to celebrating tiny milestones and helping through difficult moments — every bit of support became part of this journey.

Thank you for trusting handmade art, supporting creativity, and helping this little crochet world continue to grow.
